# FrutGo Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: January 1, 2026**  
**Last Updated: August 15, 2026**

FrutGo ("we," "our," or "us") operates the FrutGo fresh fruit, produce, and organic delivery application and platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use our mobile application, web application, and associated delivery services.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By accessing or using FrutGo, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to be bound by all the terms outlined below. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not download, register, or use FrutGo.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register for an account, place an order, interact with customer support, or participate in promotional activities. The information collected includes:

* **Personal Identification Details**: Full name, email address, telephone/mobile number, and account password.
* **Delivery & Location Data**: Delivery street addresses, apartment numbers, landmarks, postal/pincode, and optional GPS coordinates to ensure accurate route calculation and doorstep delivery.
* **Order & Transaction History**: Details of fresh fruit crates, vegetables, and salad subscriptions purchased, order timestamps, and delivery notes.
* **Notification Tokens**: Device tokens (FCM tokens) and WhatsApp phone numbers used solely to deliver real-time order confirmations, status updates, doorstep arrival alerts, and delivery receipts (via Notification Options 1, 2, and 3).
* **Technical & Device Information**: IP address, browser or app version, operating system, device identifiers, and diagnostic crash logs to maintain system security and uptime.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for legitimate business and operational purposes, including:

* **Order Processing & Fulfillment**: To process your fresh fruit and produce orders, assign delivery partners, calculate optimal dispatch routes, and deliver items to your doorstep.
* **Real-Time Communication**: To send instant in-app toasts, sound/vibration alerts, FCM push notifications, and WhatsApp updates regarding your order status.
* **Account Management**: To maintain your user profile, manage your saved delivery addresses, and authenticate your login sessions securely.
* **Customer Support**: To respond to inquiries, resolve delivery issues, and process feedback or ratings.
* **Platform Security**: To detect, prevent, and mitigate fraudulent activities, unauthorized access, and policy violations.

---

## 3. Data Protection and Security Measures

We implement robust administrative, technical, and physical security safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction:

* **Encrypted Transmission**: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS protocols.
* **Server-Side API Security**: Sensitive keys and database operations are strictly handled on secure server-side environments (`/api/*`), preventing unauthorized client-side exposure.
* **Access Controls**: Access to customer personal data is strictly restricted to authorized personnel and verified delivery partners solely for order fulfillment purposes.
* **Data Retention**: We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or comply with legal obligations.

---

## 4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to third parties. We may share your information only under the following circumstances:

* **Delivery Partners**: Assigned drivers receive necessary details (Name, Delivery Address, Mobile Number) strictly for the purpose of completing your delivery.
* **Service Providers**: Trusted third-party cloud infrastructure and messaging gateways (such as Firebase and WhatsApp Business API webhooks) that assist in operating our app under strict confidentiality agreements.
* **Legal Requirements**: When required by law, subpoena, or governmental regulation to protect our rights, safety, or property.

---

## 5. Your Data Rights & Choices

You retain full control over your personal data:
* **Access & Update**: You can view and update your profile details and delivery address book directly within your FrutGo account profile.
* **Account Deletion**: You may request complete deletion of your account and associated personal data by contacting our support team.
* **Notification Preferences**: You can enable or disable push notifications and alert channels through the app's Notification Hub.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

FrutGo does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we will delete such information promptly.

---

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational or legal changes. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the updated policy within the app or on our official GitHub repository hosting page.
